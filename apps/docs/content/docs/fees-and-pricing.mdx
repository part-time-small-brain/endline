## What you pay

The firm price you sign is all that leaves your balance for a buy, spread included.

| Other cost | Paid by |
|---|---|
| NEAR Intents fee, currently 0.0001% of each amount given up ([formula](./developers/sdk.mdx#why-trade-legs-arent-mirror-images)) | Market maker. You pay exactly the quoted price and receive exactly your size |
| Gas for trades and collects of $1 or more | Our relay. You don't need NEAR |
| NEAR wallet setup and deposits | You, as a normal NEAR transaction |
| Cross-chain deposits | You, at 1Click's fee for the route |

## How a quote works

There is no order book. While you edit, the ticket shows the market makers' current prices, refreshed every 5 seconds: the lowest price to buy and the highest to sell back, for the option you picked. A market without a price isn't offered. When you buy, the relay runs a 0.3 to 1.5 second auction among connected market makers (today, only ours). The lowest price to buy wins, or the highest to sell. Both sides settle in one NEAR Intents transaction, or neither does.

- The countdown ends 3 s before the market maker's deadline, so the trade can still land on-chain.
- Near the end, the app fetches a new price. It swaps it in if better or within 0.5% of the price you accepted, and asks you if worse, so refreshes can't add up. It never accepts a worse price for you.
- Open quotes reserve market maker inventory, so together they can't exceed your balance. A new quote cancels your unsigned one.

The ticket's "Sell back now $X" is what the market maker would pay for the same option right away. The gap to what you pay is the round-trip cost. The firm quote is that price times your size, unless the market moved or a risk limit or the $1 minimum applies.

## How prices are set

Our market maker prices each Up or Down as two options, one at the price to beat and one at the cap, using Black-76 with Deribit's implied volatility and price for the same expiry. When Deribit doesn't list a strike or expiry, it interpolates the nearest listed Deribit expiry's volatility curve.

| Our market maker today | Value |
|---|---|
| Spread | The worst of both options' volatilities moving 2 points, and each moving 1 point the opposite way, plus what the price can move in the quote's 25 s life, which matters most close to expiry |
| Cost of locked collateral | 7% a year on the USDC locked (the most the option can pay) |
| Minimum price | $1. It won't buy back options worth less, and won't sell for as much as the option can pay |
| Largest single trade | $10 of max payout, and $10 per market across all buyers, while the contract is unaudited |

It stops quoting at the sell-back cutoff (never later than 30 minutes before expiry, when the settlement average starts), on stale prices or balances, at a risk limit or when out of inventory. Other market makers can set different terms.

## Sizes

The smallest buy is the asset's [minimum size](./developers/contract.mdx#underlyings) in the contract, so the relay will collect any of your options. It pays the gas when the win is $1 or more; smaller wins are [collected from a NEAR wallet](./collecting.mdx), which pays it. You can sell back anything you hold that is worth the $1 minimum price.
