## 1. Connect a wallet

| Wallet | Examples | One-time setup |
|---|---|---|
| NEAR | Meteor, Intear, Nightly (HOT on mainnet) | "Enable": one transaction that lets the wallet sign trades on NEAR Intents |
| Ethereum | MetaMask, Rabby, any browser wallet | None |

## 2. Add funds

Pick a token and chain and send it to the deposit address shown. It arrives as USDC. Failed or short deposits are refunded to your NEAR Intents balance. From a NEAR wallet, it's one transaction.

Fund before you trade: you only get prices your balance covers.

## 3. Pick a market

The Trade screen opens in Lite: one sentence. Tap a slot to change when it ends (your time zone), above or below, the price to beat (with the % move), and what you pay: $10, $25, $50, $100 or any amount. Lite shows Max loss, a gross payout multiple, an estimated chance the final price ends above or below the price to beat, "win up to" the most the option can pay (the cap, not profit), "Full payout if BTC ends above/below $cap", and "Sell back now $X" ([round-trip cost](./fees-and-pricing.mdx#how-a-quote-works)). A market maker caps what one trade can pay out, so a long shot buys only up to that cap; Lite and Pro then say "One trade pays out at most $X, so this costs $Y."

Pro, in the header next to Lite and Tap, switches to the chart ticket: same markets, with Breakeven and "If BTC ends above $X" labeled next to Max loss.

Tap shows the same markets as a grid. Rows are prices to beat, columns are end dates, and the price line runs into a dashed line at today's price. Cells above it are Up and cells below it are Down (an option you hold keeps its cell), and each shows how many times your stake it pays at most. Pick a stake, then tap a cell: it opens the same confirm sheet as Lite, with a firm price to sign. A long shot buys only up to the market maker's cap on one trade's payout, so it costs less than your stake; its multiple is what that buy pays. A cell you hold shows what it's worth now and your profit or loss, and glows while the price is on its winning side. In the last 30 minutes before an end, while the final price is being averaged, that column turns into the final round: it can't be tapped, and your cells there show roughly what they'd pay at the average so far. After the end they show what it looks like they pay, then the proposed price, then the result. Once the price is final, a bet that made money gets a card you can share. Switch the panel under the grid to Free call to play without money: tap up to 10 cells of the next round (the next 08:00 UTC end, until 30 minutes before it) for free, each scored in points at the final price, with streaks and a leaderboard. You need $1 in your balance to play, and none of it is spent.

## 4. Buy

1. Tap Buy. The first time, your wallet asks for a sign-in signature. It moves no funds and lasts 24 hours.
2. Sign the firm price before its countdown ends. You get the option and pay in one step, or nothing happens. If the price expires while you sign, you aren't charged.
3. Optionally sign once more for auto-collect, so a win is paid without you coming back ([Collecting](./collecting.mdx)).

How the price is made: [Fees and pricing](./fees-and-pricing.mdx).

## After you buy

| When | What you see |
|---|---|
| Before the end | What you paid, value now and P&L. Sell back until 30 min before the end |
| Ended | "Waiting for the final price", then the proposed price and when it becomes final |
| Final | Paid automatically, a Collect button, or "Ended $0" |
