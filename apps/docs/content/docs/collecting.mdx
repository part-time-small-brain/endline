description: Winning options pay out automatically with auto-collect, or with one tap on Collect. Both are gasless for payouts of $1 or more.

## Two ways to get paid

| | Auto-collect | Collect button |
|---|---|---|
| You sign | Right after buying in the app | After the final price is set |
| Sent | 1 to 2 min after the price is final | From 1 min after the price is final |
| Later | Nothing to do | Tap "Collect $X" in the app |

Our relay sends both and pays the gas when the payout is $1 or more. The payout goes to your NEAR Intents balance.

Collecting returns your option to the contract for its payout. Auto-collect is that instruction signed in advance, valid until 7 days after the end. The relay holds it until the price is final, since sending it earlier would fail.

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| You win $1 or more | Paid in USDC, at most what the cap pays |
| You win under $1 | Collect it from a NEAR wallet, which pays the gas; auto-collect skips it, and an EVM wallet can't collect it in the app |
| You lose | Nothing to do. The app shows "Ended $0" |
| You sold back | The relay drops your auto-collect |
| The relay refuses auto-collect | The app says so. Use Collect once the price is final |
| Options you didn't buy in the app | No auto-collect; use Collect |
| No auto-collect, never came back | The payout waits in the contract and doesn't expire |

## Relay limits

The relay only pays gas for an exercise of our own option tokens (1 to 10 per collect, each at least the minimum size) after the price is final, with a total payout of at least $1 (collect several positions together to reach it). Smaller payouts are collected from a NEAR wallet. Each signed instruction goes on-chain once, and each IP address gets 30 requests per minute.

If the transfer to your NEAR Intents balance fails, the amount is recorded as claimable in the contract. Anyone can retry it with `withdraw_claimable`, and it always goes to you. An EVM wallet can't sign NEAR transactions, so ask us and we retry it for you. Payouts only come from the collateral locked for that market.
