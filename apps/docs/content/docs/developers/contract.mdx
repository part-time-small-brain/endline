NEP-245 multi-token contract with per-series collateral accounting. Rust, near-sdk 5.29. Source: `contracts/options`.

| Network | Account |
|---|---|
| Mainnet | `opt.endline.near` (intents: `intents.near`), no access keys |
| Testnet | `opt6.near-options-dev.testnet` (intents: `intents.near-options-dev.testnet`) |

## Init

```rust
new(owner_id, intents_id, quote_asset, quote_decimals, dispute_window_sec, oracle_delay_sec, timing: Option<Timing>)
```

`oracle_delay_sec` (60 s to 7 days, fixed at deploy; 6 h on mainnet, 60 s on testnet) is how long `add_oracle` and `set_oracle_wasm` wait before they take effect. Until then the old oracle set and WASM stay in force, and `get_pending_oracle_changes` shows what is coming. `remove_oracle` is immediate.

`Timing` is fixed at deploy and has no setter.

| Field | Mainnet default | Testnet |
|---|---|---|
| `period_sec` / `offset_sec` (expiry grid) | 86400 / 28800 (daily 08:00 UTC) | 60 / 0 (every minute) |
| `write_cutoff_sec` | 1800 | 60 |
| `oracle_window_sec` (OutLayer requests until, manual proposals from) | 21600 | 600 |
| `max_horizon_sec` | 180 days | 7 days |
| `dispute_window_sec` (60 s to 1 day) | 3600 (mainnet value; required init argument) | 120 |

## Series and token ids

`create_series(underlying, strike, width, expiry, kind)` is permissionless and payable. Attach NEAR for storage (the series plus the empty supply and balance rows its first write fills); the excess is refunded. The series records its creator (`created_by`) and what it paid (`storage_paid`).

`delete_series(series_ids)` (anyone, 1 to 20 ids, also while paused) deletes each series that is 7 days past its expiry and where no holder could still be paid anything, and returns the ids it deleted; the rest are skipped. No holder could be paid when there are no open longs or shorts (`long_open`, `short_open`, counted on every write and close, through intents or not), or when, at the final price, `floor(long_open × intrinsic / unit)`, `floor(short_open × (width − intrinsic) / unit)` and `floor(min(long_open, short_open) × width / unit)` are all 0. A floor of a total is at least the sum of each holder's own floor, so this covers every holder's rounding. A series where any holder could still be paid anything, even one unit, stays. It refunds the creator the storage the delete frees, never more than they paid. Settlements and the per-expiry listing row stay, so a price can't be proposed twice. A deleted id can't come back: its expiry is in the past. Tokens of a deleted series are worthless: they can't be exercised, redeemed or withdrawn, though they can still move between intents accounts, and `mt_balance_of` reads them as 0.

Every series is capped: a call (Up) pays `S−K` and a put (Down) pays `K−S`, each at most `width`. The cap is `K + width` for calls and `K − width` for puts.

- `underlying` must be whitelisted (`add_underlying`).
- `expiry` on the timing grid, in the future, within the horizon.
- `strike` and `width` in quote units per one whole underlying, each a positive whole cent. Puts need `width < strike`.
- The id is derived, never passed in: `{UNDERLYING}-{D}{MON}{YY}-{STRIKE}_{CAP}-{C|P}`, like Deribit's combo names. Fractional prices use `d` as the decimal point (`2650d5`). Expiries not at 08:00 UTC add the time: `BTC-24OCT26T0712-110000_112000-C`. The same strike with another width is another series.

| Token | Id | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Long (the option) | series id | `BTC-27NOV26-110000_112000-C` |
| Short (writer's claim on leftover collateral) | series id + `-SHORT` | `BTC-27NOV26-110000_112000-C-SHORT` |
| Inside intents | `nep245:<contract>:<token id>` | `nep245:opt6.near-options-dev.testnet:BTC-27NOV26-110000_112000-C` |

Option token decimals are the underlying's `decimals` from `add_underlying`. One whole token is one contract on one whole underlying.

## Math

`unit = 10^underlying_decimals`. `K` strike, `W` width and `S` settlement price, all in quote units per whole underlying. `n` option amount. `I = min(max(S−K, 0), W)` for calls, `min(max(K−S, 0), W)` for puts.

| | Calls and puts |
|---|---|
| Collateral to write `n` | `ceil(n·W / unit)` |
| Options for collateral `c` | `floor(c·unit / W)` |
| Burn pair refund | `floor(n·W / unit)` |
| Long payout | `floor(n·I / unit)` |
| Short payout | `refund − ceil(n·I / unit)`, at least 0 |

Collateral and every payout are in the quote asset (USDC). Guarantees, tested: `long + short ≤ pair refund ≤ collateral`, a price past the cap pays exactly what the cap pays, and splitting a position never pays more than closing it at once. u128 with U256 intermediates; overflow panics. Shared test vectors: `testdata/vectors.json`.

## Entry point: `mt_on_transfer`

Users reach the contract only through a signed intents `transfer` with `msg`, which makes `intents.near` call `mt_on_transfer`. Calls from anyone else are rejected. `sender_id` (the signer) owns any output, a panic refunds the signer, and a call takes at most 10 tokens.

| `msg` | Tokens sent | Effect | Returns |
|---|---|---|---|
| `{"action":"write","series_id":"…"}` | Exactly one: the quote asset (USDC) | Mint `n` long + `n` short into the sender's intents balance | Unused collateral |
| `{"action":"exercise"}` | Long tokens | Pay long payouts | Zeros |
| `{"action":"redeem"}` | Short tokens | Pay short payouts | Zeros |
| `{"action":"burn_pair"}` | `[long, short]` of one series, equal amounts | Pay the pair refund, any time | Zeros |

Writes close `write_cutoff_sec` before expiry. Exercise and redeem need a final price.

```json
{"intent":"transfer","receiver_id":"opt6.near-options-dev.testnet","tokens":{"nep141:usdc.near-options-dev.testnet":"2000000000"},
 "msg":"{\"action\":\"write\",\"series_id\":\"BTC-27NOV26-110000_112000-C\"}","min_gas":"100000000000000"}
```

## Other methods

| Method | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `exercise(token_id, amount)`, `redeem(...)`, `burn_pair(series_id, amount)` | Holder on the contract's own ledger | 1 yocto. Pays into the caller's intents balance |
| `withdraw_claimable(owner_id, asset)` | Anyone | 1 yocto. Retries a failed payout, always to `owner_id` |
| `retry_delivery(owner_id, token_ids)` | Anyone | 1 yocto, max 10 tokens. Resends options whose deposit into `owner_id`'s intents balance failed or came back unused (`get_undelivered`). Until then they sit in the contract's intents ledger row. Panics for tokens of a deleted series |
| `storage_deposit(account_id?, registration_only?)` | Anyone | NEP-145 subset. The attached NEAR, minus its own record, becomes `account_id`'s storage credit (default: the caller). It pays for new balance rows when options leave intents to this ledger. Can't be withdrawn; `registration_only: true` is rejected |
| `request_settlement(underlying, expiry)` | Anyone | Attach ≥ 0.01 NEAR, 300 TGas (less is refused). From expiry + 5 min until expiry + `oracle_window_sec` |
| `propose_settlement(underlying, expiry, price)` | Oracle account | 1 yocto. Manual fallback. Needs a listed series at that expiry, only from expiry + `oracle_window_sec`, once per key |
| `override_settlement(underlying, expiry, price)` | Owner | 1 yocto. Only before `final_at`, keeps `final_at` |
| `set_oracle_wasm(oracle_wasm: {outlayer_id, url, sha256})` | Owner | 1 yocto. Pins the oracle WASM from `now + oracle_delay_sec`: lowercase sha256, `https://` url. A new call replaces a pending WASM and restarts the delay |
| `add_oracle(account_id)` | Owner | 1 yocto. The account can propose from `now + oracle_delay_sec` |
| `delete_series(series_ids)` | Anyone | 1 to 20 ids. Deletes those 7 days past expiry that owe nothing ([above](#series-and-token-ids)), skips the rest, returns the deleted ids. Refunds go to each series' creator, one transfer and callback (about 10 TGas) per creator |
| `add_underlying`, `remove_oracle`, `set_paused` | Owner | 1 yocto |
| `propose_owner(owner_id \| null)` | Owner | 1 yocto. Replaces any earlier proposal; `null` withdraws it |
| `accept_owner()` | Proposed owner | 1 yocto. Ownership moves only now, so a typo can't lose it and a multisig or DAO proves it can call before it owns |
| NEP-245 `mt_transfer`, `mt_batch_transfer`, `mt_transfer_call`, `mt_batch_transfer_call` | Holders | ≥ 1 yocto plus storage for new balance rows. A row that empties stays at zero, so a row is paid for once. From `intents.near` (an intents `mt_withdraw`) the receiver's storage credit pays instead. If it's short, `mt_batch_transfer` fails and `mt_batch_transfer_call` answers nothing used, so intents refunds either way. Max 10 tokens per batch. No approvals. Plain transfers to `intents.near` or this contract are rejected: use `mt_transfer_call` / `mt_batch_transfer_call`. Into `intents.near` the `msg` must be empty or an account id (a plain deposit) |

Pause blocks writes and new series only. Exits are never blocked.

### Withdrawing options to this ledger

An intents `mt_withdraw` of option tokens to an account with no balance row for them needs storage credit for that account. Without it the withdraw doesn't happen and intents refunds the tokens, with or without `msg`. Put `"storage_deposit": "10000000000000000000000"` (0.01 NEAR, paid from the signer's intents wNEAR) in the `mt_withdraw` intent, or call `storage_deposit` first. A new account with a long and a short row takes about 450 bytes (0.0045 NEAR) at the longest account id; later withdrawals into existing rows are free.

## Views

`get_config` (includes `pending_owner_id`, `oracle_delay_sec`), `get_oracle_wasm` and `get_oracles` (in force now), `get_pending_oracle_changes`, `get_underlyings`, `get_series` (with `long_open`, `short_open`, `created_by`, `storage_paid`), `deletable_series(series_ids)` (the ids `delete_series` would take now), `list_series(from_index, limit ≤ 200)` (every series not deleted; a delete moves the last series into the gap, so page with `list_series_by_expiry`), `list_series_by_expiry(from_expiry, after_id, limit ≤ 200)` (series with `expiry ≥ from_expiry` in `(expiry, id)` order; for the next page pass the last one's `expiry` and `id` as `from_expiry` and `after_id`), `get_settlement(underlying, expiry)`, `get_claimable(account_id, asset)`, `get_undelivered(account_id, token_id)`, NEP-145 `storage_balance_of(account_id)` (`total` and `available` are both the credit left), and NEP-245 `mt_balance_of`, `mt_batch_balance_of`, `mt_supply`, `mt_batch_supply`, `mt_token`.

`mt_supply` includes retired tokens: options closed through intents stay in the contract's intents balance and are never burned. Open interest is the series' `long_open` and `short_open`.

## Events

- NEP-245 `mt_mint`, `mt_burn`, `mt_transfer` (`standard: "nep245"`, `version: "1.0.0"`).
- `endline` `1.0.0`: `series_created`, `write`, `exercise`, `redeem` and `burn_pair` (each with its USDC `payout`), `settlement_proposed`, `settlement_overridden`, `payout_failed` (`owner_id`, `asset`, `amount`: now claimable), `delivery_failed` (`owner_id`, `token_ids`, `amounts`: now in `get_undelivered`, resend with `retry_delivery`), `series_deleted` (`series_id`, `created_by`, `refund`, `dust`: the rounding left in the series, which the contract keeps), `storage_refund_failed` (`account_id`, `amount`: the NEAR stays in the contract).
