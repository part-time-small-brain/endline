## Can I lose more than I pay?

No.

## How is this different from a yes/no "Up or Down" market?

A yes/no market pays a fixed amount. Here you choose the price to beat, and the payout grows with how far past it the final price lands, up to a cap two price steps out, about 1.2–3% past it ([How it works](./how-it-works.mdx)).

## Who is on the other side of my trade?

Our market maker, for now. It locks the full payout in the contract before selling, so your payout never depends on it. It doesn't hedge.

## When does a market end?

Daily at 08:00 UTC, shown in your time zone. Testnet also lists a 4-hourly end.

## What if the final price is wrong?

It can't become final until the 1 h dispute window ends, and the owner can override it before then ([Settlement](./settlement.mdx#dispute-window)).

## Why does Tap's final round say "about"?

The app estimates the average from Deribit's index, minute by minute, filling the minutes still to come with today's price. The real final price is the median of 7 exchanges' averages ([Settlement](./settlement.mdx#the-final-price)), so the two can differ a little. Within 0.1% of the price to beat it says "too close to call".

## What are free calls?

Free calls in Tap move no money, but you need $1 of USDC in your balance to play; none of it is spent. You call up to 10 cells of the next round, one each (at most 100 calls a round from one network): the next 08:00 UTC end, open until its final price starts being averaged 30 minutes before. A call stakes 100 points at the cell's multiple, up to 25×, and scores at the final price: at or past the cap a 5× call pays back 500, so +400; between the price to beat and the cap it pays back part of that and can still lose points; short of the price to beat it's −100. A call wins only if it scores above 0, and a streak is the number of rounds in a row with a win. Points only, no prizes. The leaderboard shows NEAR Intents account ids, which are public. A NEAR account needs its one-time Enable first, as for buying.

## Can I sell before the end?

Yes, with Sell back, until 30 minutes before the end, at a price of at least $1.

## Do I need NEAR for gas?

No. The relay pays gas for trades and collects. Only NEAR wallet setup and deposits are normal transactions.

## What if I forget to collect?

The payout waits in the contract and doesn't expire. Tap Collect any time, or turn on auto-collect to skip that.

## Why is the payout capped?

So every option can be fully backed with a little USDC. The most an option can pay is locked before you buy, and the cap keeps that amount small: about $5 of collateral locked for every $1 of premium you pay, instead of a whole coin behind every Up. Every payout is USDC.

## Which wallets work?

NEAR and Ethereum wallets ([Quickstart](./quickstart.mdx#1-connect-a-wallet)).

## Is my position private?

No. Option balances on NEAR Intents are public.
