## In five steps

1. Put USDC in your NEAR Intents balance. You can fund it from any chain.
2. Pick an end time for BTC, a price to beat and what you pay. Up pays if the final price ends above the price to beat, Down if below, and the further past it, the more you get, up to a cap two price steps out.
3. Sign once at a firm price. You don't need a gas token.
4. At the end, the final price is the median of 7 major exchanges.
5. If you were right, the payout lands in your balance automatically. If not, you lose what you paid and no more.

Up is a call and Down is a put with a capped payout: European, cash-settled and fully collateralized in USDC. [How it works](./how-it-works.mdx) has the math.

Your USDC, options and payouts are balances in `intents.near`, the NEAR Intents contract. We never hold your funds, and there is no bridge of ours in between.
