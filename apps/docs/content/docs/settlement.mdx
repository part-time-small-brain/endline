## The final price

Each exchange's 1-minute closing prices are averaged over the 30 minutes before the end. The final price is the median of those averages.

| Exchanges | Quoted in |
|---|---|
| Coinbase, Kraken, Bitstamp, Bitfinex | USD |
| Binance, OKX, Bybit | USDT, converted at a 30-minute USDT/USD average from Coinbase and Kraken |

- An exchange counts only with at least 25 of its 30 one-minute prices, and at least 3 exchanges must count on 08:00 UTC markets (which have the Deribit check below), 4 on other expiries, so one bad exchange can't move the median.
- If the two USDT/USD rates are missing, more than 0.2% (20 bps) apart, or outside 0.97 to 1.03, the USDT exchanges drop out.
- On 08:00 UTC markets, no price is set if Deribit's delivery price is more than 0.2% (20 bps) from the median, or if Deribit doesn't answer (we retry every 10 minutes). Deribit alone never sets the price.
- Selling back and new options stop 30 minutes before the end, when the averaging window opens.

Exact rules: [Oracle WASM](./developers/oracle.mdx).

## Timeline

| Step | When |
|---|---|
| Market ends | Once a day at 08:00 UTC, shown in your time zone |
| Selling back closes | 30 min before the end |
| Price requested | From 5 min after the end, by our settler every 10 min until a price is in. Anyone can request it too |
| Manual price allowed | From 6 h after the end |
| Dispute window | 1 h |
| Auto-collect sent | 1 to 2 min after final |

The app shows "Waiting for the final price, usually about 1 h", then the proposed price and when it becomes final.

## Who computes it

[OutLayer](https://outlayer.fastnear.com/docs/getting-started) runs the oracle WASM pinned in the contract by sha256, inside a TEE (Intel TDX), and the contract stores the price from OutLayer's callback. You also trust OutLayer's operator: the TEE is checked off-chain, not by the contract.

## Dispute window

A proposed price becomes final when its dispute window ends. Until then our monitor checks it against Deribit every minute and alerts if it's more than 0.2% off or missing, and the owner can override it. The owner can't move the end of the window or change its length, but it can stop a price from being proposed at all ([Safety and risks](./safety-and-risks.mdx#what-the-owner-can-and-cant-do)).

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What happens |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 3 exchanges (4 on a market not ending at 08:00 UTC), median and Deribit more than 0.2% apart, or no Deribit price on an 08:00 market | No automatic price. The oracle account proposes one by hand once the manual window opens |
| OutLayer down or timing out | Our settler retries every 10 min, and anyone can. If none succeeds within 6 h, manual proposal |
| Wrong price proposed | The owner overrides it inside the dispute window |

Until a price is final, the market is pending and nothing pays out. There is no fallback payout formula.
